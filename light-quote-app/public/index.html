How it works

From "how much?" to booked, without the site visit.

  1. 1

    They tap your link

    From your ads, your website or a text. A short quiz asks what they want lit: roofline, trees and shrubs, or the full house.

  2. 2

    They see their own home lit up

    They add a photo and get a preview of their house in warm white, bright white and multicolour, with a price range beside it.

  3. 3

    You measure from above

    The request lands on your phone. Trace the roofline on a satellite view and the quote fills itself in at your own rates.

  4. 4

    You text the quote, they book

    One tap sends it. They accept on their phone and pick an install date. You show up to hang lights, not to give a price.

Questions

Before you call.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. Lumiquote is a one-time licence. You buy it once and it is yours.

How much is it?

Call or text (647) 883-0113. It is a single payment, and we will walk you through it on a live demo so you can see it working first.

Will my customers know it is Lumiquote?

No. They see your logo, your colours and your phone number. Our name is not on their pages.

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. It is a link that opens in the browser on any phone or computer.

How accurate is the price they see?

They see a range based on their home size and your rates. You confirm the exact price after you trace the roofline, so the final quote is always yours.

What if the customer is not home to take a photo?

The quiz offers to open Street View of their address so they can screenshot the house and upload that. They can also skip the photo and you still get the request.

Can I change my prices mid-season?

Yes. Rates, minimums, colours and how the price is shown can all be changed from your dashboard whenever you like.

I am not a tech person. Is it hard to set up?

We set it up for you with your logo, colours and prices. You get a link to share and a dashboard to check.

Want to see it on your own house?

Call or text and we will send you a live link. Run your own address through it, then decide.
